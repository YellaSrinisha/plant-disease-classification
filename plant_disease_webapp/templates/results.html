🎯 Why Ensemble? — The All-Rounder Argument

SVM achieves the highest field accuracy. But the ensemble is the better all-rounder — it outperforms every individual model on the full combined test set (both lab + field images), which is the real deployment scenario where images of unknown quality arrive.

📊 Full Accuracy vs Field Accuracy per Model

A model that scores high on both bars is a better all-rounder.

Conclusion
The ensemble is the best choice when lab-quality and field-quality images arrive together — highest combined accuracy and most stable field predictions. SVM is the stronger choice if deployment is restricted to field photos only and the 1.4% accuracy trade-off is acceptable.
